Qingxuan Wang

I am a postdoctoral researcher and assistant research fellow at Nankai University, working with Professor Ding Wang.

My research focuses on authentication and applied cryptography. I study the design and security analysis of password-based multi-factor authentication protocols, with particular interest in whether their security assumptions and proofs remain sound under realistic threats.

I received my Ph.D. degree in Information Security from Nankai University in 2024.

Email/ Scholar/ ResearchGate/ DBLP

Research

My research interests include password-based and multi-factor authentication, protocol security, applied cryptography, and post-quantum security. My work has examined failures in authentication security proofs, the practical relevance of ephemeral secret leakage models, quantum-resistant two-factor authentication, and lattice-based cryptographic applications.

I aim to develop authentication mechanisms whose security guarantees are both formally grounded and meaningful in real-world deployments.
